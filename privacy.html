Privacy Policy — Adelia Gmail Automation

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Adelia Gmail Automation is a personal automation tool operated by Eduardo Gomes.

Data accessed

With the account owner’s permission, the tool accesses Gmail messages already in the Spam folder. It may process sender address, subject line, message identifiers and Gmail labels solely to identify spam and manage Gmail filters.

How data is used

Data is used only to review Spam messages, create or update Gmail spam filters, move confirmed spam messages to Gmail Bin, and produce local operational summaries for the account owner.

Data sharing

Gmail data is not sold, rented, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Access is limited to the account owner’s private automation environment.

Data retention and security

Gmail remains the primary data store. The tool may retain minimal local operational reports and filter metadata needed to verify results. OAuth access tokens are stored locally with restricted permissions. The account owner can revoke access at any time from Google Account security settings.

Contact

This is a private, personal-use application. Privacy requests can be made through the support contact shown during the Google authorisation process.
